# Rooms

## Replacing a room's key

Replacing the key gives the room a new key and hands it to everyone Yaparr has seen in it. Banned people, and anyone who only kept the old invite, can no longer find the room or talk in it. If someone on the list should not have the new key, ban them first.

Only the person who made the room can do this. See [Moderation](../moderation.md#replacing-the-room-secret).

## The GIF shelf

Pictures every member of a room can send from the GIF button. Anyone can add; you can remove your own, and the person who made the room (and its admins) can take down any. Click a picture for its options. Your own shelf, **Mine**, stays on your device. See [Pictures and GIFs](../pictures-and-gifs.md).

## The soundboard

Short sounds that everyone in the room can play into a voice call from the Soundboard button. Add a WAV, MP3 or OGG file; a longer one is cut to 20 seconds. It is made as loud as the other sounds, and everyone in the room can use it. Sounds are added in the room's settings; the soundboard itself is where sounds are played, renamed and deleted.
