# Backups and accounts

## Backups

A backup is a copy of everything Yaparr keeps for you: your identity key, rooms, room keys, message history, pictures and settings. You use it to bring your account back on a new device if you lose this one.

Keep it somewhere safe. It holds your account key: anyone with the file can pretend to be you and read your rooms. Lock it with a passphrase and the file is safe to keep anywhere, such as a cloud drive or an email to yourself; without the passphrase it is unreadable.

To bring it back, choose **Restore a backup** on the first screen of a device with no account. That device becomes another device of you, so the old one can stay in use.

## Starting on a new device

When you open Yaparr for the first time it asks whether you already have an account:

- **Link this device**: join the account of a device you still use. Your name, rooms and blocks carry over, and people see you as one person.
- **Restore a backup**: bring back an account from a backup file, for a new or reinstalled device.
- **Restore from Yaparr Cloud**: the same, from the account you keep in sync on [Yaparr Cloud](yaparr-cloud.md). You need its address, your account there and your Sync key.
- **Start fresh**: make a new account.

## Logging out

**Log out** removes your account, rooms, history and settings from this computer and takes you back to the first-start screen. Your other devices keep the account.

See [Devices and accounts](../devices-and-accounts.md) for how several devices share one account.
