# Soundboard

[Documentation](README.md)

Every room has a soundboard: a list of short sounds that any member can add and that everyone in the voice channel can play into it. The Desktop app has a **Soundboard** button in the voice controls (shown while you are in voice) that opens the soundboard over the app, like the settings (Escape, the cross or a click outside puts it away): a tile for every sound, with a coloured badge and who added it; click a tile to play it for everyone in voice (it lights up when anyone plays it), or press its number: a row of ten keys, 1 to 9 and then 0 (the top row or the number pad), sits above the tiles, and each key plays the sound you put on it. **Drag a sound from the tiles onto a key** to put it there (what was on the key goes off the keys; a sound is on one key only), **drag a key onto another** to swap them, drag a key back onto the tiles to empty it, or right-click a key to take its sound off or to start again from the first ten sounds. Each tile shows the key it is on. The arrangement is yours alone, kept in your settings by room (`AppSettings.SoundboardSlots`; nobody else sees it, and it does not travel to your other devices): until you change it the keys hold the first ten sounds in the order they are listed, and once you have, they stay as you left them (a sound that is removed leaves its key empty; new sounds do not move anything). The board has the keyboard when it opens, so the number keys work at once; the keys do nothing while the cursor is in the search box, where digits are typed, and a letter typed on the board moves the cursor to the search box (when there is one) and is typed there, right-click for a private preview, rename or delete (your own sounds; an admin can rename or delete any). A search box appears once there are many sounds. **Pop out** moves the soundboard to a window of its own to keep beside the call (on another monitor, say), and **Show in the app** puts it back; the app remembers which you used last. Sounds are added in the room's settings (the **⋯** menu, then **Room settings**, then **Soundboard**): **Add a sound** takes a WAV, MP3 or OGG file, a name, and for a file longer than 20 seconds the point to start from, with a preview. Right-click a person in the voice list, or use the bell button next to them, to mute their soundboard sounds: whatever they play, whoever added it, is not heard by you (their voice is not affected), which is for when one person keeps pressing sounds. It is the person who is muted, not a device: the mute is kept under their account, so it holds on all their devices (a mute made before, under one device's id, moves to the account the first time it is known). Muting the sounds someone added is not a thing, because it is who plays them that matters. The soundboard has "Mute all soundboards" and a volume slider; these are remembered between runs.

- **Sounds.** `SoundClip.FromPcm(samples, rate, channels)` or `SoundClip.FromWav(file)` turns a sound into Yaparr's own small format (20 ms Opus frames, 48 kHz mono, about 32 kbps) and makes it as loud as other sounds, so one loud file does not blast the channel. A sound is at most 20 seconds (`SoundClip.MaxSeconds`); there is no other limit on how many sounds there are or how big the list gets. The person adding a sound converts it, and everyone else only reads this format and never an MP3 or WAV from a stranger. MP3 and OGG files need decoding to samples first, which the library does not do (the Desktop app does it with NLayer and NVorbis).
- **The list.** `room.AddSound(name, clip)`, `room.Sounds`, `room.RenameSound`, `room.RemoveSound`, `room.GetSound(id)` (to preview) and `room.SoundsChanged`. Each entry is signed by whoever added it and passes around the room like presence, the newest statement about an entry winning; a removal is kept as a note so it cannot be undone by someone who still has the old entry. Only the person who added a sound can remove it or change what it plays; the owner and the admins of the room can also rename any sound (a signed order, `rensound`, kept with the other moderation orders: the sound shows the newer of that name and its owner's own), and delete any. A joining member is sent the whole list, and a restart keeps it when `ChatNodeOptions.SoundboardDirectory` is set (the list is sealed on disk with the history key when there is one, tied to the room's file name; a plain list from an earlier version is read and written back sealed, and one that cannot be opened is kept aside as `.unreadable` while the list is fetched again from the room). Sounds of people you have blocked are hidden.
- **The files.** A sound's audio is stored by its hash, like a picture, and downloaded from the member who added it or any other member that has it. Sounds you do not have yet are fetched in the background when the list changes, when you join voice, and every 15 seconds while you are in voice.
- **Playing.** `room.PlaySound(id)` sends a small signed message ("play this sound", a few hundred bytes) to the room and plays the sound for you. Everyone in the voice channel who has the sound plays it from their own copy, so a press costs the same however many people listen and the sound is the same file for everybody. It returns false if you are not in voice, are deafened or do not have the sound. `room.SoundPlayed` tells the app who played what. A sound starts when the play message reaches a listener, so someone on a slower path hears it a little later than someone on a faster one (the message is relayed through the room like any other); that is fine for effects and is not meant for anything that has to line up.
- **Muting.** Sounds are mixed on their own track, separate from voices. `session.SetSoundboardMute(person, true)` silences one person's soundboard without muting their voice (`person` is any of their devices or their account, and all their devices are silenced; what you play yourself never is); `session.SoundboardsMuted` silences everyone's (yours too); `session.SoundboardVolume` sets how loud sounds are compared with voices (0.8 by default). Being deafened silences sounds as well. The sounds go through the speaker mix that echo cancellation listens to, so your microphone does not send them back.
- **Limits.** Only people in the voice channel can play sounds into it, and a play message must be signed by that person. A listener drops a person's sound if it comes sooner than `SoundboardMinInterval` (3 seconds) after their last one.
- **Limits on the list.** A room holds at most 100 sounds (`SoundLimits.PerRoom`), also enforced on what other members send, so nobody can fill every member's disk; each sound is already limited to 20 seconds. There is no limit per person (in practice one or two people add clips). The oldest removed entries of a person beyond 50 are forgotten. The file of a removed sound (by its owner, by an admin, or replaced) is deleted at once unless another sound is the same file, and the week-old file clean-up at startup no longer deletes the files of sounds that are still on a soundboard (it used to, because sounds are stored with pictures). Sounds already over the limit stay, only new ones are refused.
