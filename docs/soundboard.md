# Soundboard

[Documentation](README.md)

Every room has a soundboard: a list of short sounds that any member can add and that everyone in the voice channel can play into it. The Desktop app has a **Soundboard** button in the voice controls (shown while you are in voice) that opens a window of tiles: click a tile to play it for everyone in voice, right-click for a private preview, rename or delete (your own sounds), or to mute the person who added it. **Add a sound** takes a WAV, MP3 or OGG file, a name, and for a file longer than 20 seconds the point to start from, with a preview. Next to each person in the voice list is a bell button that silences only their soundboard, and the window has "Mute all soundboards" and a volume slider; these are remembered between runs.

- **Sounds.** `SoundClip.FromPcm(samples, rate, channels)` or `SoundClip.FromWav(file)` turns a sound into Yaparr's own small format (20 ms Opus frames, 48 kHz mono, about 32 kbps) and makes it as loud as other sounds, so one loud file does not blast the channel. A sound is at most 20 seconds (`SoundClip.MaxSeconds`); there is no other limit on how many sounds there are or how big the list gets. The person adding a sound converts it, and everyone else only reads this format and never an MP3 or WAV from a stranger. MP3 and OGG files need decoding to samples first, which the library does not do (the Desktop app does it with NLayer and NVorbis).
- **The list.** `room.AddSound(name, clip)`, `room.Sounds`, `room.RenameSound`, `room.RemoveSound`, `room.GetSound(id)` (to preview) and `room.SoundsChanged`. Each entry is signed by whoever added it and passes around the room like presence, the newest statement about an entry winning; a removal is kept as a note so it cannot be undone by someone who still has the old entry. Only the person who added a sound can rename or remove it. A joining member is sent the whole list, and a restart keeps it when `ChatNodeOptions.SoundboardDirectory` is set. Sounds of people you have blocked are hidden.
- **The files.** A sound's audio is stored by its hash, like a picture, and downloaded from the member who added it or any other member that has it. Sounds you do not have yet are fetched in the background when the list changes, when you join voice, and every 15 seconds while you are in voice.
- **Playing.** `room.PlaySound(id)` sends a small signed message ("play this sound", a few hundred bytes) to the room and plays the sound for you. Everyone in the voice channel who has the sound plays it from their own copy, so a press costs the same however many people listen and the sound is the same file for everybody. It returns false if you are not in voice, are deafened or do not have the sound. `room.SoundPlayed` tells the app who played what.
- **Muting.** Sounds are mixed on their own track, separate from voices. `session.SetSoundboardMute(person, true)` silences one person's soundboard without muting their voice; `session.SoundboardsMuted` silences everyone's (yours too); `session.SoundboardVolume` sets how loud sounds are compared with voices (0.8 by default). Being deafened silences sounds as well. The sounds go through the speaker mix that echo cancellation listens to, so your microphone does not send them back.
- **Limits.** Only people in the voice channel can play sounds into it, and a play message must be signed by that person. A listener drops a person's sound if it comes sooner than `SoundboardMinInterval` (3 seconds) after their last one.
- **Limits on the list.** A room holds at most 100 sounds (`SoundLimits.PerRoom`), also enforced on what other members send, so nobody can fill every member's disk; each sound is already limited to 20 seconds. There is no limit per person (in practice one or two people add clips). The oldest removed entries of a person beyond 50 are forgotten. The file of a removed sound (by its owner, by an admin, or replaced) is deleted at once unless another sound is the same file, and the week-old file clean-up at startup no longer deletes the files of sounds that are still on a soundboard (it used to, because sounds are stored with pictures). Sounds already over the limit stay, only new ones are refused.
