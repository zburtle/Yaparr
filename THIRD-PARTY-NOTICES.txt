FFmpeg (the FFmpeg developers), downloaded on first use
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Yaparr does not contain FFmpeg and does not link to it. When you share or watch a screen it offers to download an LGPL
build of FFmpeg (without GPL components such as x264) into your own application data folder and runs it as a separate
program. FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later; the licence text is kept
next to the downloaded program (LICENSE.txt), together with a note of where the source code and build scripts are
(https://ffmpeg.org and https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds). The build includes libvpx (BSD-3-Clause, Google).
